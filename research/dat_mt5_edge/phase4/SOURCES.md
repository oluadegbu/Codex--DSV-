## Recommended acquisition sequence

1. Recover and validate existing primary objects first (completed for eleven EURUSD objects). They cannot supply a meaningful OOS sample.
2. For long FX history, prioritize a scoped Dukascopy primary export with a reviewed definition/decoder chain; use independently sourced ticks for cross-checks. Tick Data is a licensed alternative if cost is approved. HistData is a secondary comparison, not a provenance shortcut. USDJPY requires JPY PNL conversion rather than borrowing EURUSD tick value.
3. Obtain a bounded historical quote estimate for raw ES/MES, NQ/MNQ, YM/MYM contracts before any acquisition. Keep the [Phase 3 cost gate](../phase3/ACQUISITION_PLAN.md). Never use adjusted continuous prices for fills; predeclare rolls based only on completed prior sessions, include both legs' costs and exclude transition sessions.
4. BTC public trade archives can extend trade-series research but do not resolve quote certification. Do not pay for more trade-only OHLCV expecting historical spreads.

For a requested basket, require one written total covering symbols, date bounds, schema, raw definitions, applicable exchange/provider license, expected bytes, transfer, storage, update obligation, taxes and a hard authorized dollar ceiling. No supported all-in quote currently exists, so no purchase recommendation is authorized. Public-page research and local offline validation can continue automatically; credentials, contractual enrollment, purchases and broker access cannot.
